Haan, Rocket Rush ko ek 2D endless runner / arcade survival game ki tarah design kar sakte ho. Isme user rocket ko control karta hai, obstacles avoid karta hai, coins collect karta hai, aur jitni der survive kare utna score badhta hai.

Rocket Rush — Detail Logic
1) Main idea
Rocket continuously आगे move karta rahega.
Screen par asteroids, lasers, meteor fragments, space mines aate rahenge.
Player ko left-right movement se dodge karna hoga.
Coins / energy cells collect karke score aur upgrades milenge.
Jitna long survive, utna high score.
2) Game loop
Game start
Countdown
Rocket move begin
Obstacles spawn hona start
Player dodge + collect
Difficulty dheere-dheere increase
Collision hua to game over
Score, coins, distance, best score show
3) Controls
Left / Right: rocket ko lane ya free movement me shift karna
Boost button: short speed burst
Shield button: temporary protection
Mobile me:
swipe left/right
tap boost
tap shield
4) Scoring system
Distance score: jitna aage, utna score
Coin score: har coin ka alag value
Combo bonus: continuous coins collect karne par multiplier
Near-miss bonus: obstacle ke bahut paas se bachne par extra points
Survival bonus: time-based points
5) Difficulty system

Game time ke saath hard hota jaye:

obstacle speed increase
obstacle spawn gap reduce
moving asteroids add hona
fake safe gaps kam hona
occasional special hazards aana
6) Obstacle types
Asteroid: basic moving obstacle
Debris field: multiple small objects
Laser beam: timed barrier
Mine: touch hote hi damage
Black hole zone: movement slow
Comet streak: fast cross obstacle
7) Power-ups
Shield: 1 hit se bachata hai
Magnet: coins auto collect
Boost: temporarily speed up and invincible feel
Double coins: score multiplier
Repair pack: health restore
8) Health / lives

Simple system rakh sakte ho:

Rocket ke paas 3 health bars
Light hit = 1 health minus
Full collision = instant damage
Health 0 = game over

Ya:

One-hit mode for hardcore gameplay
9) Win condition

Is type ke game me usually “win” nahi hota, balki:

best distance
highest score
daily challenge complete
mission clear
